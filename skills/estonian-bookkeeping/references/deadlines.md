# Estonian filing deadlines

Source: [EMTA — Tax rates](https://www.emta.ee/en/private-client/taxes-and-payment/declaration-income/tax-rates), [EMTA — Tax types](https://www.emta.ee/en/business-client/taxes-and-payment).

## Monthly cycle

| Day | Return | What |
|---|---|---|
| **10th** | **TSD** | Income tax & social tax declaration (PIT withheld, social tax, UI, II pillar, fringe benefits annex 4, representation annex 5) |
| **14th** | **Intrastat** | Trade in goods within the EU — if over the annual threshold |
| **20th** | **KMD** + **KMD INF** | VAT return + annex listing invoices ≥ €1,000 net per partner |
| **20th** | **VD / VDP** | Recapitulative statement for intra-EU supplies of goods and services |

All for the previous calendar month.

If a deadline falls on a weekend or public holiday, it shifts to the next business day.

## Quarterly / annual

| Period | Return | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Quarterly | **OSS** | End of next month (electronic services to consumers in EU) |
| Monthly (above threshold) | **IOSS** | End of next month (import scheme for low-value goods) |
| Annual (financial year) | **Majandusaasta aruanne** (annual report) | **6 months** after year-end (typically 30 Jun for calendar-year companies) |
| Annual | **Income tax annual return (Form A)** for natural persons | 30 Apr |
| Annual | **Form E** for FIE (sole proprietor) | 30 Apr |

## Holiday-affected dates 2026

Estonian public holidays that affect filing: 1 Jan, 24 Feb, Good Friday (3 Apr), Easter Sunday (5 Apr), 1 May, Pentecost (24 May), 23 Jun (Võidupüha), 24 Jun (Jaanipäev), 20 Aug, 24–26 Dec.

When the 10th, 14th, or 20th falls on one of these or a weekend, the deadline moves to the next business day.

## Payment vs declaration

The deadlines above are for **declaration**. Payment of taxes due is on the same day. If the declaration is filed late, interest accrues from the original due date.

## Penalties

- Late declaration: administrative fine, scaled by delay and by taxpayer category. EMTA typically warns once before fining.
- Late payment: statutory interest (currently 0.06%/day = ~21.9% annualised).

## Excise

Excise duties (alcohol, tobacco, electricity, fuel) increased by **+10% across the board** from 1 Jan 2026, with further increases legislated for 2027–2028. Excise returns are monthly (15th); only relevant to businesses dealing in excise goods.

## Reporting calendar — recommended workflow

For a typical small Estonian OÜ:

1. **By month-end** — ensure all invoices and bank statements are imported into Merit Aktiva.
2. **By the 5th** — reconcile bank, payroll, and any open documents.
3. **By the 9th** — finalize TSD figures (PIT, social, UI, II pillar, fringe benefits).
4. **10th** — file TSD via e-MTA.
5. **By the 19th** — finalize VAT and INF.
6. **20th** — file KMD and VD via e-MTA.
7. **End of FY + ≤6 months** — file annual report via the äriregister (Company Registration Portal).
