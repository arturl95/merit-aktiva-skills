# Payroll taxes 2026

Sources: [EMTA — Tax rates](https://www.emta.ee/en/private-client/taxes-and-payment/declaration-income/tax-rates), [Grant Thornton — 2026 changes](https://www.grantthornton.ee/en/insights1/which-tax-changes-will-enter-into-force-in-2026/), [Grant Thornton — Basic exemption 2026](https://www.grantthornton.ee/en/insights1/calculating-the-basic-exemption-from-2026/).

## Personal income tax

**Rate: 22%**, flat, withheld at payroll. The 24% plan for 2026 was cancelled in December 2025.

## Basic exemption (mahaarvamine) — 2026 reform

| Group | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| General | **€700** | €8,400 |
| Old-age pensioner | **€776** | €9,312 (automatic, no application needed) |

**The "tax hump" is gone**: from 1 Jan 2026 the basic exemption no longer tapers with income. Flat €700/month for everyone, regardless of earnings.

The employee must submit a written application (Mahaarvamise kohaldamise avaldus) to apply the exemption at source. Default is no exemption.

## Social tax (sotsiaalmaks)

**Rate: 33%** on gross remuneration. **Paid by the employer.**

| Item | 2026 value |
|---|---|
| Monthly minimum base | **€886** |
| Minimum monthly social tax liability | **€292.38** |

The minimum kicks in whenever any payment to a person triggers social-tax liability — including small or part-time payments and board-member fees. Pro-rate for the first/last month of employment.

The minimum base typically rises annually; verify on the EMTA tax-rates page each January.

## Unemployment insurance (töötuskindlustus)

| Side | Rate |
|---|---|
| Employee | **1.6%** (withheld) |
| Employer | **0.8%** (paid on top of gross) |

Not levied on:
- Board member fees (juhatuse liikme tasu) — neither side.
- Persons of pensionable age — employee side waived.

## Funded pension — II pillar (II sammas)

Employee chooses the contribution rate:

| Rate | Status |
|---|---|
| **2%** | Default since 2024 |
| **4%** | Optional, on written application |
| **6%** | Optional, on written application |

The state adds 4% on top from social tax to the employee's II pillar account regardless of the employee's chosen rate. Applications submitted Jan–Nov take effect the following calendar year. Contribution rate is visible in the employment register; payroll must follow the rate flagged by EMTA.

Employees who paused contributions in 2020–2023 can resume from 2024 onward.

## Worked example — €1,000 gross salary, 2% II pillar, basic exemption €700 applied

### Employer cost

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Gross salary | €1,000.00 |
| Social tax (33%) | €330.00 |
| Unemployment insurance employer (0.8%) | €8.00 |
| **Total employer cash out** | **€1,338.00** |

### Employee receives

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Gross salary | €1,000.00 |
| − UI employee (1.6%) | −€16.00 |
| − II pillar (2%) | −€20.00 |
| Taxable for PIT after exemption | (1,000 − 16 − 20 − 700) = €264 |
| − PIT (22% of €264) | −€58.08 |
| **Net to employee** | **€905.92** |

Note: the precise PIT base interaction with II pillar varies by exact employer payroll rules; the above approximates within ±€2 of EMTA's payroll calculator.

## Minimum wage (1 Apr 2026)

- Monthly: **€946**
- Hourly: **€5.67**

The annual minimum-wage figure changes each 1 April; cross-check before payroll runs.

## Board member fees (juhatuse liikme tasu)

Special handling, frequently mis-booked:

- Subject to **22% PIT** (withheld) and **33% social tax** (employer).
- **NOT subject to unemployment insurance** (neither side) — board membership is not employment under the Employment Contracts Act.
- **NOT subject to II pillar** for the same reason.
- Basic exemption applies if elected.
- **Triggers the €886 minimum social-tax base** even on small payments — paying €100 still costs €292.38 in social tax. Be careful with token monthly fees.
- Non-resident board members: social tax not due if an A1 certificate from another EU/EEA state is held.
